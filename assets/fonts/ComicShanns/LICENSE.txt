Comic Shanns Regular (1.3.0)
Source: the Excalidraw client fork, excalidraw-team/packages/excalidraw/fonts/ComicShanns/.
Merged from the client's unicode-range woff2 subsets (ComicShanns/*.woff2); family name set to "Comic Shanns". Converted to TTF so the server-side renderer (resvg) and text metrics can use it; glyphs, metrics and kerning are unchanged.
